A planner built for ADHD brains, by Doublard Digital, Jersey, Channel Islands.

Contact us

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email harry@doublarddigital.com and we'll get back to you within 2 working days.

You can also send feedback from inside the app: Settings → Send Feedback.

Subscriptions

ADHD Life Premium is billed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change plan, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Changed phone? Tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and data.

If you can't sign in, email harry@doublarddigital.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it within 30 days.
