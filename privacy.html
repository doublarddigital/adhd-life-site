Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

ADHD Life is operated by Doublard Digital, Jersey, Channel Islands ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information ADHD Life may collect, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ADHD Life may collect or process information including:

• Name, email address and account/profile information.

• Authentication information needed to create and maintain your account.

• Tasks, subtasks, reminders, habits, routines and calendar information you choose to add.

• Focus sessions, productivity information, app preferences and settings.

• Information you voluntarily enter when using AI-powered features.

• Optional medication reminder, wellbeing or routine information that you choose to provide.

• Subscription status and purchase entitlement information.

• Support requests, feedback and communications you send to us.

• Technical information reasonably required to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. Account and Sign-In Information

ADHD Life may support sign-in methods such as email, Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple. If you use a third-party sign-in provider, we may receive basic account information necessary to authenticate you and create or maintain your ADHD Life account. The information available to us depends on the provider and the permissions you grant.

3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

• Create, authenticate and maintain your account.

• Store and synchronise tasks, habits, routines and preferences.

• Provide reminders, notifications, focus tools and productivity features.

• Personalise parts of the app based on your settings and choices.

• Provide AI-powered planning, task breakdown, routine and coaching-style features.

• Manage premium access and subscription entitlements.

• Maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve ADHD Life.

• Respond to support requests and important service communications.

• Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI-Powered Features

ADHD Life may offer AI-powered features such as task breakdowns, daily planning, routine suggestions, focus assistance and productivity coaching. Information you submit to an AI feature may be sent to an AI service provider where necessary to generate the requested response.

Do not submit passwords, payment card information, highly confidential information, or other information that is unnecessary for the AI feature. AI-generated responses can be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be treated as medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

5. Health and Medication-Related Information

ADHD Life may include optional features relating to habits, medication reminders, wellbeing or routines. Any such information is entered voluntarily by the user and is intended to support organisation and reminders.

ADHD Life is a productivity and organisational application. It is not a medical device, healthcare provider, emergency service or substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Users should follow instructions from qualified healthcare professionals regarding medication and health decisions.

6. Service Providers

We use trusted service providers to operate ADHD Life, including Supabase (account authentication and cloud database), OpenAI (AI-powered features), RevenueCat (subscription management), Apple and Google (sign-in and app distribution), and email/support services.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and are subject to their own contractual and privacy obligations.

7. Payments and Subscriptions

Where ADHD Life offers paid subscriptions or in-app purchases through an app marketplace, payment processing is generally handled by the relevant marketplace or payment provider. ADHD Life may receive information such as subscription status, product purchased, renewal status or transaction identifiers, but we do not need to receive your full payment card details for marketplace purchases.

8. Notifications

If you enable notifications, ADHD Life may send task reminders, habit reminders, medication reminders, focus-related prompts or other notifications you have requested. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings and, where available, within ADHD Life.

9. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. However, no internet-connected service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide ADHD Life, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

11. Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to complain to an applicable data protection authority.

For users in Jersey, data protection rights may arise under Jersey data protection law. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

12. Account and Data Deletion

Where required, ADHD Life will provide a way for users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security or legitimate operational reasons.

13. Children's Privacy

ADHD Life is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required and has not been obtained. Age requirements should be clearly stated in the final App Store listing and onboarding flow and must match the final product configuration.

14. International Processing

Some service providers may process information outside Jersey. Where applicable, we will take appropriate steps required by data protection law to protect personal information when it is transferred internationally.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ADHD Life develops or when legal, technical or business requirements change. The latest version will show its effective or last-updated date. Where appropriate, material changes may also be communicated within the app or through another reasonable method.
